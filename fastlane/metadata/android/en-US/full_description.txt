A wave-based 2D shooter with roguelite elements. Play as a nanobot fighting pathogens inside a human body.

Features:
• Wave-based combat with 30+ enemy types (viruses, bacteria, parasites)
• 10+ weapons: pistols, shotguns, sniper rifles, rocket launchers
• Power-ups: slow time, heal, screen blast, invincibility
• Upgrade system with augments between waves
• Touch controls with virtual joysticks
• 30+ language localizations
• Achievement tracking
• 5-minute runs perfect for mobile play

Controls:
• Left joystick: Move
• Right joystick: Aim (auto-fires)
• Buttons: Power-ups, weapon switch

Survive for 5 minutes to win!
